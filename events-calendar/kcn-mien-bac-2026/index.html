Event Overview

With FDI capital into Vietnam reaching a record $6.74 billion in Q1/2026 (+34% year-on-year), the need for direct connectiondirect connection between foreign investors and Northern Vietnam industrial park infrastructure is at an all-time high.

Northern Vietnam Industrial Park Investment Connect 2026 is a specialised B2B forum organised by HOUSELINK, bringing together all parties in the industrial investment ecosystem: FDI/DDI investors seeking locations, IP management boards from Hanoi to the northern provinces, and a network of verified contractors and suppliers.

Unlike traditional conferences, this event is designed around genuine business connections: each delegate is advised on 1-1 meeting pairings matched to their investment profile and specific needs.

Why you should attend?

  • Meet directly with 30+ Industrial Park Management Boards from Hanoi, Hai Phong, Bac Ninh, Bac Giang, Quang Ninh, Thai Nguyen, Hung Yen and other northern provinces
  • Purposeful 1-1 connections with FDI investors from Japan, Korea, the US and EU seeking factory locations within the next 6–12 months
  • Receive the exclusive report: "Northern Vietnam IP Market H1/2026 — Occupancy, Rental Rates and Relocation Trends"
  • Join the session: Semiconductor, AI/Data Center and green industry investment trends in Northern Vietnam
  • Access the HOUSELINK Database of 120,000+ factories and a network of 2,000+ verified contractors
  • 100% Free — limited to 200 delegates to ensure connection quality
