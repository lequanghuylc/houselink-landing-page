About HOUSELINK
6 questions
What is HOUSELINK and what do you do?+

HOUSELINK is an industrial ecosystem platform connecting investors, contractors, and suppliers in Vietnam’s manufacturing and industrial construction market. Founded in 2010, HOUSELINK operates one of Vietnam’s largest industrial databases, covering 120,000+ factories and production sites.

We provide six core service groups:

  • 01 Market Research — Market research and investment advisory
  • 02 Site Selection — Location search and industrial park (KCN) selection
  • 03 Business Setup — Company incorporation and investment legal support
  • 04 Project Implementation — Project execution support and contractor screening
  • 05 ESG Platform — ESG infrastructure and reporting for industrial zones
  • 06 Find Project — Project opportunity matching for contractors and suppliers
See all services →
Who do you serve?+

HOUSELINK supports three main groups:

  • Investors / FDI: Multinational groups and local firms building factories, warehouses, or expanding manufacturing in Vietnam
  • Industrial contractors: General contractors and subcontractors for factories, steel structures, and MEP seeking FDI projects
  • Suppliers: Companies providing steel, cement, MEP equipment, construction materials, and O&M services

Today, 2,000+ companies across 30+ countries participate in the HOUSELINK ecosystem.

What is the HOUSELINK Database?+

HOUSELINK Database is a large dataset of Vietnam’s industrial and construction market, including factories, industrial parks, investors, verified contractors, and suppliers.

Data is consolidated from government sources, industrial zone authorities, licensed datasets, and HOUSELINK field research. Our research team updates continuously (weekly and monthly cycles depending on the dataset).

Do you work with foreign companies?+

Yes. Since 2010, HOUSELINK has supported clients from 30+ countries, including Japan, Korea, the US, Taiwan, Singapore, and EU markets. We can deliver bilingual Vietnam–English reports and contracts upon request.

How are you different from typical consultants?+

Our advantage comes from:

  • Owned data: 120,000+ factories built and updated over 15 years
  • Ecosystem access: direct connection to enterprises, contractors, and industrial parks
  • Integrated ESG: end-to-end support from measurement to reporting and green energy pathways
