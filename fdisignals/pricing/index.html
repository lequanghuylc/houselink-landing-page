Frequently asked questions

Why is monthly billing not available from the start?

Signals run on a 6-24 month cycle. One or two months is too short to see value, and it undermines organisations that commit long term.

Why are investor contacts not provided?

When many parties contact the same investor, that investor stops replying to everyone. HOUSELINK coordinates and presents at most three options - which is why investors keep listening.

Do unused connection requests roll over?

No. Requests are granted at the start of each quarter and do not accumulate. Requests declined for low match are refunded automatically.

Not sure which plan fits?

A HOUSELINK specialist will review recent signals that match your organisation and recommend the right plan.
