Limits

What FDIsignals
does not promise

Not every signal becomes a project

Some companies weigh Vietnam and choose elsewhere, or postpone. What the platform guarantees is that you know while there is still time to act.

It does not replace your promotion team

A signal is a starting point. Persuading the investor still depends on people, infrastructure and negotiation.

No direct investor contacts

A deliberate choice. When many parties contact the same investor at once, that investor stops replying to everyone.
