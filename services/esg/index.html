Implementation Process

From assessment to
international certification

The ESG roadmap is specifically designed for each business — tailored to industry, scale and supply chain requirements.

🔍 01 · ESG Current State Assessment ›
Review all manufacturing operations, identify Scope 1, 2, 3 emission sources and gaps against target standards. Initial diagnosis free via the Glassdome platform — no prior system investment required.
🎯 02 · Build Roadmap & Targets ›
Establish an emission baseline, set science-based emission reduction targets (SBTi) and an annual action roadmap — tailored to the business's scale, industry and Net Zero objectives.
📊 03 · Inventory & Measurement ›
Collect real data from machinery via the Glassdome platform (no average estimation factors), calculate GHG emissions per ISO 14064-1 and product carbon footprint per ISO 14067, prepare detailed reports.
✅ 04 · Independent Verification ›
Connect with internationally accredited verification bodies (LRQA, Bureau Veritas, SGS, TÜV Nord) for verification under ISAE 3000 / ISO 14064-3 — the process integrated within the Glassdome platform significantly reduces audit time and cost.
🏆 05 · Certification & Reporting ›
Complete international ESG certifications, publish sustainability reports to GRI/SASB standards and prepare all CBAM/CSRD compliance documentation — ensuring uninterrupted EU exports and meeting requirements of Apple, Samsung, Nike and Unilever.
