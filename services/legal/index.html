Implementation Process

Clear Roadmap -
concrete results

Every application is managed strictly with a clear timeline - ensuring businesses are not delayed by administrative procedures.

🎯 01 · Initial Advisory & Assessment ›
Analyse investment objectives, business type, industry and location to determine the most suitable legal roadmap. Forecast timeline, budget and potential risks from the outset.
🗺️ 02 · Procedure Planning ›
Build a detailed roadmap with timeline, responsible authority and documents to prepare for each step - from IRC, ERC to construction, fire safety, environmental and operating permits.
📂 03 · Application Preparation ›
Support for drafting, translation (Vietnamese–English–Chinese–Japanese–Korean) and completing applications to meet the exact requirements of each competent authority. Application review before submission to avoid supplementary requests.
📬 04 · Submission & Tracking ›
Submit applications and track processing progress at government agencies, coordinating timely responses to supplementary requests. Regular progress updates for clients via email/Zalo.
✅ 05 · Receive Results & Post-Permit ›
Hand over permits, guide on post-permit obligations (periodic reporting, renewal, compliance) and support for subsequent procedures such as tax registration, labour and social insurance.
