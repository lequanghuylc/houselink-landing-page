Implementation Process

Clear Roadmap —
concrete results

Each project is individually designed by industry, scale and priority criteria — from database screening to a complete recommendation report.

💬 01 · Define Selection Criteria ›
Discuss requirements on floor area, industry, geographic location, infrastructure, labour and land lease budget. Build a priority criteria matrix (must-have vs. nice-to-have) as the screening basis.
🗄️ 02 · Rapid Database Screening ›
Mining HOUSELINK Database 400+ KCN spanning 34 provinces to build a shortlist of 5–8 potential sites in a few days — saving weeks of manual searching.
📊 03 · Preliminary Analysis & Scoring ›
Compare sites using a customised scoring tool, eliminating sites that do not meet minimum criteria. Result: a list of the 3 best sites for field survey.
📍 04 · Field Site Survey ›
Directly visit 3–5 selected sites, assess infrastructure conditions and meet with industrial park management. Collect real data not available in secondary reports — lease terms, incentives, construction progress.
🔬 05 · Due Diligence & Report ›
In-depth technical analysis (power, water, waste treatment), commercial (rent, operating costs) and legal (permits, zoning). Present a comparative report and final recommendations with a negotiation roadmap.
