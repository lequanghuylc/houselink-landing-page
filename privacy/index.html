2. Definitions

  • Personal data: digital data or information in any other form that identifies or helps to identify a specific individual. Information about companies, projects and industrial parks is not personal data, except for any part linked to a specific individual.
  • Sensitive personal data: data in the categories listed in Decree No. 356/2025/NĐ-CP, for example health, biometric and location data, card information and bank transaction history, and data tracking a person's behaviour when using online services.
  • Data subject, "you": the individual to whom the personal data relates.
  • Processing of personal data: any operation performed on data, such as collection, analysis, storage, provision, public disclosure, transfer and deletion.
  • Processor: an organisation that processes data on behalf of HOUSELINK under a contract, for example server and email service providers.
  • Member: an organisation or individual with an account on the HOUSELINK platform, whether free or paid.
  • Contact person: an individual working at a company, project owner, contractor or industrial park who is listed in HOUSELINK's database.

5. Company information and contact persons on the platform

The HOUSELINK platform provides information about companies, projects and industrial parks. We do not sell, rent or transfer personal contact information, whether under a service package or on request.

  • Company information (name, enterprise code, address, business lines, general email, switchboard number) is not personal data.
  • Legal representatives: only the full name and job title are displayed, exactly as published on the National Business Registration Portal, together with the source and date of update. The legal representative's phone number, personal email, identity document number and address are not displayed.
  • Other contact persons: their full name and job title are displayed only if they consent. Without consent, the platform displays only a department or a generic role, for example "Purchasing department".
  • Individuals' phone numbers and email addresses are not displayed to members. Members who wish to make contact send a connection request through HOUSELINK; we forward the request to the company's general email address or to a person who has agreed to receive connection requests.

If you find your information on the platform, you can email info@houselink.com.vn with a link to the page containing that information.

7. HOUSELINK AI and automated processing

HOUSELINK AI answers questions about projects, companies and industrial parks within the permissions of your account.

  • The question box states clearly that you are asking an artificial intelligence assistant.
  • The context sent to the model contains no personal contact information. HOUSELINK AI does not answer questions aimed at looking up or compiling information about an individual.
  • Please do not enter your own or other people's personal information in your questions.
  • Questions are separated from your account before being stored and are deleted after 90 days.
  • Data sent to the model is not used by the provider to train models, under the terms of the service plan that HOUSELINK uses.
  • Insights reports prepared with the assistance of artificial intelligence are labelled as such and are reviewed and approved by a person before publication.

We do not use automated processing to make decisions that have legal effects on individuals. Rankings and ratings on the platform apply to companies, not to individuals. You have the right to request that your data not be subject to automated processing, and to ask a member of HOUSELINK's staff to review a result generated by the system.

12. Data protection and incident handling

We apply organisational and technical measures appropriate to the level of risk to the data:

  • Encryption of connections between your browser and our servers (HTTPS/TLS); protected backups;
  • Role-based access control, under which access is granted only where needed for work; mandatory two-factor authentication for administrative staff;
  • Detailed personal contact information can be viewed only by a small number of administrators, and every view is logged;
  • Staff sign confidentiality undertakings and receive regular training;
  • Providers that process data must sign contracts containing confidentiality obligations;
  • New features that use personal data are assessed before launch.

In the event of a leak or loss of, or unauthorised access to, personal data, we take immediate action to contain it. We notify the specialised personal data protection authority within 72 hours and notify affected individuals in accordance with the law. Incident records are retained for inspection purposes.

13. Children's personal data

HOUSELINK's services are intended for organisations, companies and adults working in industrial real estate and investment. HOUSELINK does not knowingly collect personal data of persons under 18, including children (persons under 16), and does not design its services for children.

  • When registering an account, you confirm that you are 18 or older.
  • If we discover that we have collected a child's data without consent as required by Article 24 of Law 91/2025/QH15, HOUSELINK will stop processing and delete that data without delay.
  • Parents or guardians may contact us as set out in section 16 to request a review and deletion.

14. Possible risks and unintended consequences

Although we apply the measures described in section 12, no system is completely secure. Possible risks include:

  • unauthorised access resulting from cyberattacks or software vulnerabilities;
  • human error, for example sending an email to the wrong recipient or granting the wrong permissions;
  • account takeover because a password has been exposed or is reused on other services;
  • emails, messages or calls impersonating HOUSELINK for fraudulent purposes.

Consequences may include receiving spam, being defrauded or being impersonated. To reduce these risks, you should use a unique password for HOUSELINK, not share your account and sign out of shared devices.

HOUSELINK will never ask you to provide a password or OTP code, or to transfer money, by email, message or phone. Official emails are sent only from the houselink.com.vn and houselink.info domains. If you suspect that someone is impersonating HOUSELINK, please let us know as set out in section 16. If an incident occurs that may affect you, HOUSELINK will notify you as set out in section 12.
