What's New in the System

The new HOUSELINK platform introduces a redesigned investor dashboard that consolidates project tracking, document management, and real-time communication into a single unified workspace. At the core is an AI-powered site-matching engine that analyses over 200 data points — including land availability, logistics infrastructure, labour costs, and ESG readiness — to surface the most relevant industrial parks options for each investor profile.

Vietnam's industrial real estate sector is experiencing unprecedented demand, with Q1/2026 FDI inflows reaching USD 6.74 billion — up 34% year on year. The speed and accuracy of investment facilitation is now a critical differentiator. The new HOUSELINK platform is purpose-built to compress decision cycles from weeks to days, giving both investors and industrial parks operators a significant competitive advantage in a fast-moving market.
