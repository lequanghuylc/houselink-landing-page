Situated along Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street, The Marq is one of the most unique projects in the trendiest quarter of Ho Chi Minh City.

For wealthy and successful lifestyle connoisseurs, the value of a real estate project at the heart of the city centre lies not only in the developer’s reputation, its magnificent scale, unique architectural style, or luxurious amenities, but also in invisible features such as its heritage value. Nestled in a prime District 1 location, The Marq, a luxury apartment project on Nguyen Dinh Chieu street crafted by Hongkong Land and An Khang, more than meets all of these expectations.

The heritage value

It has been more than 300 years since the official decree to establish Saigon – now Ho Chi Minh City. This may not seem long, but it was more than enough to turn the city into a “must-live” destination offering the beauty of both Eastern and Western cultures, with a historical, architectural, and cultural heritage that makes anyone who has fallen in love with the city proud to be a resident.

Situated along Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street, a peaceful avenue hidden under rows of lush, ancient trees and lined with classic French villas, The Marq is a unique project in the heart of the most dynamic city in Vietnam.

Within a few minutes’ walk, you can reach historic buildings that have survived over three centuries, such as the lofty Notre Dame Cathedral, with its two soaring bell towers, built in the Romanesque architectural style; the Opera House and its flamboyant bas-reliefs and sophisticated decorative patterns; the Central Post Office with a hybrid Gothic-Renaissance style; The Continental Hotel, a witness of countless historical events that has welcomed famous figures such as French writer André Malraux, Graham Greene, and many journalists, dignitaries, and foreign traders.

Contemporary design in harmony with nature

Amidst the quiet notes that contain nostalgic vibes, The Marq bursts into song rejoicing in contemporary architectural styles blended with peaceful nature and luxurious amenities.

Leaving behind the noisy and bustling rhythms of metropolis, you will enter a private realm beginning with the gentle green of the French landscaped garden, the murmuring sound of a waterfall in the main drop-off, the warm light of modern chandeliers, and the tranquility of the beautiful onyx marble flooring and marble walls in the main lobby. All of this combines to create a resplendent, peaceful atmosphere, so that every passing second is memorable for those who appreciate the real values of life.

Stepping into your apartment will take you to the absolute privacy of a peaceful oasis without having to leave the dynamic city behind, with luxurious, sophisticated interior design combined with the smart technology of the digital era.

The ceilings soar up to 3.2 metres, with large windows creating a feeling of airiness, while the marble kitchen counter, marble floors, marble bathroom floors, and walls exude sophistication in all living spaces.

For the first time in Vietnam, each unit in The Marq features a state-of-art touch screen smart mirror powered by smart home technology, bringing a unique experience to residents, whether you need to look up the weather forecast, news updates, upcoming schedules, ask a question, setup reminders, or control other smart devices in the house.

Lifestyle privileges

Of course, for successful, sophisticated residents, a true living space is not limited to the above-mentioned factors: it must also be reflected in lifestyle privileges, including internal and external amenities. Like many luxury condo projects for elite residents around the world where lifestyle privileges provide an additional value that is not immediately apparent, The Marq will bring special experiences to its residents through The Sky Club on the terrace.

With a breath-taking view of Southeast Asia’s most dynamic city, The Sky Club is a peaceful oasis, with an overflowing pool in the central area, surrounded by the Sky Bar, Sky Dining, the Banquet Room with private kitchen, and the BBQ Bar.

For the little ones, the Kid’s Pool in the adjacent building offers room for fun, while adults can pamper themselves in the nearby jacuzzi. In the same building, you will also find an exclusive gym.

An elegant living space in the heart of the dynamic city centre with more than 300 years of heritage value: The Marq sets new standards for elite living.
