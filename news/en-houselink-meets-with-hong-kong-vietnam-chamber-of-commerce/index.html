In the morning of September 12, a working session with the Hong Kong – Vietnam Chamber of Commerce was attended by many business leaders.

The first day of the Belt and Road Summit 2019 showed some positive results.

At the meeting with Hong Kong – Vietnam Chamber of Commerce, business leaders had the opportunity to discuss the difficulties encountered, and at the same time proposed effective solutions with an aim to building a long lasting business community.

The session took place with a series of outstanding breakthrough solutions focusing on practical issues related to specific industries and markets, including topics on finance, investment, legal issues, infrastructure, technology, as well as geopolitics, risk management, human resources, and SMEs on ring roads and regions.

Joining the meeting, Mr. Nguyen Thanh Long – CEO of HOUSELINK Joint Stock Company participated in discussions with the business leaders.

Within the framework of the Belt and Road Summit 2019, HOUSELINK was pleased to welcome visitors our booth at the exhibition area. Visiting HOUSELINK’s booth within the conference, visitors had the opportunity to experience the online bidding platform – eBidding, which was launched early in the year, more specifically, the smartphone app – HOUSELINK – introduced for the first time to help contractors, suppliers and construction units access this platform anytime and anywhere.
