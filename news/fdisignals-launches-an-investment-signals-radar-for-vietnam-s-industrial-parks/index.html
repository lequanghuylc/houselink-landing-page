FDIsignals Launches: An Investment Signals Radar for Vietnam’s Industrial Parks

HOUSELINK has launched FDIsignals.com, a platform that helps industrial parks, contractors and service firms identify foreign manufacturers planning to expand production into Vietnam while they are still choosing a site.

HOUSELINK Oct 19, 2026

HOUSELINK has launched FDIsignals.com, a platform that helps industrial parks, contractors and service firms identify foreign manufacturers planning to expand production into Vietnam while they are still choosing a site.

What FDIsignals Does

FDIsignals combines a data system covering more than 145 stock exchanges and a range of specialist sources with HOUSELINK's multilingual analyst team, focusing on manufacturers in the industries and markets each client cares about. Every signal is assessed to judge how likely the company is to choose Vietnam. Across verified cases, signals appeared about 11 months before the investment registration certificate (median).

Built on HOUSELINK's Foundations

FDIsignals does not start from zero. It is built on HOUSELINK's hands-on experience in industrial park infrastructure and FDI: investment promotion advisory and market research for park developers, work alongside many of Vietnam's leading industrial parks, an infrastructure database of more than 500 industrial parks and clusters together with nationwide industrial project data, and in-depth reports on FDI by region and sector as well as on CBAM and its impact on Vietnamese manufacturers. In ESG infrastructure advisory for industrial parks, HOUSELINK has signed memoranda of understanding with BECAMEX VSIP Binh Dinh and GLASSDOME at the ESG conference it co-organised at WTC Binh Duong on 31 March 2026, a tripartite MoU with KBC and GLASSDOME at the Korea - Vietnam Green Manufacturing Value Chain Forum in Seoul in June 2026, and a tripartite MoU with Da Hop and GLASSDOME for Tan Vien Tech Valley Industrial Park on 14 August 2026.

That accumulated knowledge allows FDIsignals not only to detect signals but to judge which investors genuinely fit each park, and to support parks all the way to the site visit.

Four Goals

FDIsignals aims to help industrial parks know investors earlier and know who fits them. Every signal is matched against capability profiles of more than 500 industrial parks and clusters verified by HOUSELINK.

It also aims to make investment promotion more efficient and less costly, so teams can focus on the most promising investors.

Another goal is to give a clear view of the market and of FDI flows into Vietnam through the Monday digest and the monthly bilingual bulletin.

Finally, FDIsignals aims to bring investment promotion into a transparent, verified process. HOUSELINK does not pass on investor contacts; it reviews each request and presents at most three suitable options, reducing reliance on unverified intermediaries.

Behind these goals is a wish to help Vietnam attract projects that create quality jobs, bring good technology and respect the environment, in line with Resolution 10-NQ/TW.

Five Steps

The process begins with registration and consultation: a specialist calls within one working day to agree target industries and the right plan.

Next, the industrial park builds its capability profile by submitting a one-page template, and HOUSELINK checks each item against source documents.

The client then receives matched signals through a verified list every Monday morning, while particularly strong matches are emailed the same day.

When the client sends a connection request, HOUSELINK responds within 48 hours.

Finally, the client gets introduced to the investor through at most three options, with HOUSELINK joining the site visit.

Who It Is For

Industrial parks receive early-stage signals, before investors have chosen a site. Contractors and suppliers receive upcoming projects, timelines and work packages. Finance, legal and advisory firms receive signals filtered by industry and investor nationality. Each plan is tailored to the client's needs.

What FDIsignals Does Not Promise

Not every signal becomes a project. FDIsignals does not replace an investment promotion team, because winning an investor still depends on infrastructure, policy and people. And it does not hand out investor contacts, because when too many parties call at once, investors tend to turn everyone down.

“A factory abroad is always a multi-year plan, and that plan leaves a trail. We built FDIsignals so that Vietnam's industrial parks can read that trail earlier, meet the right investors and negotiate on value rather than price.”

Interested organisations can request a free consultation at fdisignals.com. A HOUSELINK specialist will be in touch within one working day to share recent signals relevant to them.
