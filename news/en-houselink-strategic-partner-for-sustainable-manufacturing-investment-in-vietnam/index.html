Leveraging ESG Trends to Conquer Global Markets

As the EU’s Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) is set for full implementation in 2026, and global supply chains increasingly demand transparency in carbon emissions alongside stringent ESG standards, Vietnam is emerging as a leading destination for green manufacturing investment.

Multinational corporations are no longer selecting partners and investment locations based solely on cost efficiency. Instead, priority is given to capabilities in achieving Net Zero, adopting circular economy models, and ensuring social responsibility-key factors for accessing EU and U.S. markets as well as green financing.

HOUSELINK Joint Stock Company is a pioneering firm in investment research and market intelligence in Vietnam, specializing in supporting international manufacturers in expanding their operations in alignment with ESG orientation.

  • In-depth Market Research Reports:
    Delivering comprehensive insights into investment policies, green industrial development strategies, sector trends (manufacturing, eco-industrial parks), and opportunities in sustainable supply chain relocation.
  • ESG Assessment Tailored to Vietnam:
    Providing risk–opportunity analysis related to CBAM, CSRD, and international standards; supporting businesses in selecting ESG-compliant industrial zones and integrating renewable energy and circular economy practices.
  • Proven Track Record & Strong Network:
    In 2025, HOUSELINK contributed to facilitating over 10% total of newly registered investment capital in Vietnam, through high-quality reports and a robust partner ecosystem.
