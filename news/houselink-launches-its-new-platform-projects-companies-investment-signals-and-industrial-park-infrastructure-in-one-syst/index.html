HOUSELINK launches its new platform: projects, companies, investment signals and industrial park infrastructure in one system

From 6 October 2026, HOUSELINK's new platform is live at houselink.com.vn, bringing data on projects, companies, FDI investment signals and industrial park infrastructure together on one platform.

HOUSELINK Oct 6, 2026

From 6 October 2026, HOUSELINK's new platform is live at houselink.com.vn, bringing data on projects, companies, FDI investment signals and industrial park infrastructure together on one platform.

One platform for the whole ecosystem

The new platform serves everyone involved in an industrial project: industrial park developers, investors, contractors, suppliers, and financial, legal and advisory firms.

Instead of searching in several places, users work in one system, with a single search box for projects, companies, investment signals, bid packages and reports.

Large-scale, verified data

The HOUSELINK database now holds more than 10,000 projects, including over 700 new ones in 2026; nearly 28,000 companies, with more than 9,000 verified profiles; and more than 500 industrial parks.

Every page shows when its data was last updated, so users always know how current the information is.

Core features

  • Projects: Search by the new administrative boundaries, individual industrial parks, type, stage and FDI or DDI capital; view on a map, as cards or as a table; track bid packages nearing their deadlines.

  • Investment Signals (FDIsignals): Early visibility of foreign companies preparing to invest in Vietnam, verified before reaching clients.

  • Industrial Park Infrastructure: Infrastructure and land availability for industrial parks nationwide.

  • Professional Profiles: Companies claim and update their own capability profile, reference projects and documents.

  • Reports: Market analysis with interactive charts in Vietnamese, English, Chinese, Korean and Japanese.

  • HOUSELINK AI: Ask about the data in plain language; answers stay within each account's permissions.

Members sign in at houselink.com.vn with their registered email; anyone without a password on the new platform can choose "Forgot password?"

About HOUSELINK

HOUSELINK provides advisory services and market data on industrial park infrastructure, FDI and industrial construction in Vietnam, working alongside many of Vietnam's leading industrial parks.

In ESG infrastructure advisory for industrial parks, HOUSELINK has signed MoUs with BECAMEX VSIP Binh Dinh and GLASSDOME (March 2026), a tripartite MoU with KBC and GLASSDOME (June 2026), and a tripartite MoU with Da Hop and GLASSDOME for Tan Vien Tech Valley Industrial Park (August 2026).
